What is ANIMON?

ANIMON is an anime character discovery and collectible-card experience. It randomly discovers anime characters and presents them as collectible cards.

Rarity classifications

  • MYTHIC: Extremely popular characters; the rarest tier.
  • LEGENDARY: Highly popular and widely recognised characters.
  • EPIC: Characters with substantial popularity.
  • RARE: Characters with moderate popularity.
  • COMMON: Characters with comparatively lower popularity.

Rarity is calculated from each character’s popularity (favourites) in the database. It is never randomly assigned.

What does ANIMON provide?

  • Random anime character discovery.
  • Character artwork.
  • Anime series identification.
  • Anime quotes.
  • Five elemental reveal experiences: Fire, Water, Earth, Air and Lightning.
  • Unique character identification numbers.
  • One-click PNG download of every card, in the same size on every display.
